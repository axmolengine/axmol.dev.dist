FAQ

For an Open Source Collective (OSC) contribution, sign in to your account, open Dashboard > Contributions, switch to the account used for payment, and cancel the recurring contribution to Axmol in your OSC dashboard.

For PayPal, sign in at paypal.com, then go to Settings > Automatic Payments > Manage Automatic Payments and cancel the one labeled Axmol Sponsors.

For GitHub Sponsors, visit github.com/sponsors, open Your Sponsorships, find Axmol, and choose Cancel Sponsorship from the options menu.

If you need help, contact oss@simdsoft.com and include the email address associated with your PayPal or GitHub account and your current sponsorship tier, if applicable.

Unless you request otherwise before payment, completing a sponsorship means you agree that your name and, where applicable, logo may be displayed on axmol.dev, simdsoft.com, or both, solely to confirm the sponsorship relationship and publicly acknowledge your support. To remain anonymous, contact oss@simdsoft.com before payment. You may also contact us at any time to correct or remove an existing listing.

Corporate support is handled directly by Simdsoft. Contact info@simdsoft.com for a private discussion. Support does not grant ownership or control over Axmol’s technical direction.
